How much can I save with solar?

Most homes cut their electricity bill by 80–90%. The exact number depends on system size, your usage pattern and how much shade-free roof you have. Use the calculator above for a personalised estimate.

How does the government subsidy work?

Homes in Bihar get two subsidies. The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy is up to ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW (flat ₹78,000 above 3 kW). The Bihar government adds up to ₹10,000 for 1 kW and ₹20,000 for 2 kW and above. That's up to ₹98,000 in total. You pay for the system, and after installation and inspection the subsidy is credited to your bank account. We handle both applications.

How long does installation take?

Usually 7–10 days from site survey to commissioning. Net-meter installation depends on DISCOM timelines, which we follow up on for you.

What is the payback period?

Most residential systems pay for themselves in 4–6 years through savings, after subsidy. Panels then keep generating for 25+ years.

Which brands do you install?

We are an Official Waaree Channel Partner and also install Adani and Tata panels. All are Tier-1 brands with long performance warranties.

Will solar work during power cuts?

A standard on-grid system shuts off during outages for safety. If power cuts are frequent in your area, choose a hybrid system with battery backup.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

Yes. Our local teams in Patna, Lakhisarai, Begusarai and Sheikhpura provide after-sales service and support. Ask us about maintenance plans when you get your quote.
